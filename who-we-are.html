A FAMILY ROOTED IN MINISTRY

Meet the Pastor’s Family

On October 5, 2007, in a small Harlingen apartment, a new church began with fewer than ten people. First known as Worship Ministries Church, which first grew out of Worship Ministries, a three-year-old ministry serving children and youth across South Texas. God had other plans rather just a ministry but to be called to church plant Worship Ministries Church.

Pastor Gilbert De Leon had served as a youth and children’s pastor and Co-Pastor prior to starting Worship Ministries and Worship Ministries Church. That small gathering became the beginning of a new chapter known today. In 2026, Pastor De Leon now serves as the senior pastor and founder of Worship Him Church in Harlingen, Texas.
