OUR HEART AS A CHURCH

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our beliefs

These convictions guide our worship, our relationships, and the way we serve.

01

The Bible

We believe the Bible is God’s inspired Word and a trustworthy guide for faith and daily life.

2 Timothy 3:16–17
02

Our mission

We are here to help people know God, grow in faith, and share the hope of Jesus with others.

Matthew 5:14–16
03

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is the Son of God, our Savior and Redeemer, and the center of our faith.

John 14:6
04

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is present with believers and empowers us to live transformed lives.

John 14:26
05

Prayer

We believe prayer draws us closer to God and belongs at the heart of everything we do.

Philippians 4:6–7
06

Community

We believe we grow in faith together as we love, encourage, and serve one another.

Hebrews 10:24–25
